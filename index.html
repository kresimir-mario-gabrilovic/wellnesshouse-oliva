The area

Inland calm, coast nearby

Martinski sits in the green hills of eastern Istria. Medieval Labin is up the road, the pebble beaches of Rabac below it. Marked cycling routes run through the hills, and Bike Park Rabac is by the coast.

  • Labin old town10 km
  • Rabac beaches14 km
